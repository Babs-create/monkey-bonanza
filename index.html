Monkey Bonanza

Ten jungle levels, rebuilt at random every run. Run, jump, stomp and swing from vine to vine. Grab every banana and reach the Great Banana Tree.

Field guide

Vines
Jump into one to grab it. Lean the way you are swinging to build speed, then jump to let go. Some gaps are too wide to jump.
Snakes
Land on their heads to squash them. Touch them from the side and you get bitten.
Thorn bushes
Never land on them. Jump over, or take the branches above.
Rolling coconuts
They start rolling once you get close. You can't stomp them, so jump.
Falling coconuts
Palm trees drop one when you run underneath. Keep moving or hang back.
Bees
They weave through the air in waves. Wait for a clear gap.
Spiders
They bob up and down on a thread. Run through when they are high, or wait for them to rise.
Parrots
They swoop low across the ground. Pass under when they are high, or stomp them for 200 points.
Steam vents
Rumbling means a blast of steam is coming. Stay clear until it dies down.
Snapping plants
They shiver, then snap open. Harmless while closed, painful while open.
Bats
They hang until you get close, screech, then dive at where you were standing. Keep moving.
Beetles
They patrol branches. Stomp them for 100 points.
Rival monkeys
They hurl coconuts in an arc at you. Stomp them for 150 points.
Storm clouds
A glowing mark on the ground shows where lightning will strike. Leave it before the flash.
Bosses
A boss guards the Great Banana Tree on every level. Stomp its head or throw bananas (X or Shift, bosses only) until its health bar is empty. Dodge its charge and coconuts.
Hornets
They swoop in wide loops. Wait for a clear gap.
Saw blades
They race back and forth along the ground. Time a jump over them.
Stalactites
They shake, then drop when you walk underneath. Run through fast or wait for it to land.
Frogs
They hop at you in big leaps. Stomp them for 100 points.
Electric fences
A flickering beam means it is about to switch on. Jump it while it is off.
Spore mushrooms
They shake, then puff a poison cloud. Wait for it to clear.
Scorpions
They creep, then dash when you get close. Stomp them for 100 points.
Cannons
They fire iron balls along the ground. Jump the shot.
Spike traps
Small spikes poke up as a warning, then shoot out. Cross while they are down.
Bouncing coconuts
They bounce back and forth along the ground. Jump over the low point.
Gorillas
They charge when you get close. Stomp them for 200 points.
Falling rocks
A growing shadow on the ground means a rock is about to drop. Move off it.
Mud pits
Mud halves your speed and shortens your jump. Cross it with a running start.
Spiked balls
They swing over river gaps. Time your jump to pass behind them.
Piranhas
Ripples in the water mean one is about to leap across the gap.
Wind gusts
Leaves streaking sideways mean a gust is coming. It shoves you back mid-air, so wait it out.
Crumbling branches
Pale branches fall away soon after you land on them. Keep moving. They grow back.
Crocodiles
Bubbles in the river mean one is about to launch out of the water.
Logs
Floating logs slide back and forth across river gaps and carry you with them.
Mangos
Absorb one hit, then they are used up.
Totems
Touch one to save your spot. You restart there after a fall.
Bananas
Golden bunches are worth 5. Every 50 bananas earns an extra life.
